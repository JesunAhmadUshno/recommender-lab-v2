## 4. Per-hypothesis evidence

**H1, REFUTED.** Popularity does not beat a uniform random ranker under the
temporal protocol on either KuaiRec arm. Arm T: Popularity 0.000073 against
Random 0.001425. Arm F: Popularity exactly 0.000000 against Random 0.006544,
because the most-exposed train-window items never appear as first-time
positives in the test window. Popularity's recommendations are also a
degenerate set: on Arm F its top-1-percent item share is 0.9998 and its item
coverage is 0.0069, the same items for everyone (`popularity_report.csv`).

**H1a, CONFIRMED.** Per-user NDCG difference ItemKNN minus Popularity on
Arm T KuaiRec: mean 0.000870, one-sided 95 percent lower bound 0.000618,
Holm-adjusted p 0.0 over 10,000 bootstrap draws, n = 6,638 users.

**H1b, CONFIRMED.** EASE minus ItemKNN: mean 0.000500, lower bound 0.000139,
Holm-adjusted p 0.021, n = 6,638.

**H2a, REFUTED.** On the corrected Arm F, exact ordering (descending):
iALS, EASE, Random, ItemKNN, Popularity. Sampled-100 ordering: Random,
ItemKNN, iALS, EASE, Popularity. The frozen rule required a complete
reversal (Kendall tau exactly -1.0); the measured tau is 0.2. The strong
form of the claim fails. The weaker fact underneath it does not: sampled
metrics put a uniform random ranker first and the true best model third,
with mean absolute error 0.0943 NDCG points against the exact values.

**H2b, CONFIRMED.** The bias-corrected estimator cuts the sampled mean
absolute error from 0.0943 to 0.0262 (condition: at least a 30 percent
reduction) and its ordering (iALS, EASE, Random, ItemKNN, Popularity) equals
the exact ordering, while the sampled ordering does not (flip condition).
Both frozen conditions hold.

**H3a, CONFIRMED.** Random-split mean NDCG against temporal exact NDCG,
five seeds each: KuaiRec ItemKNN 0.0563 against 0.000943 (about 60x), EASE
0.0615 against 0.001443 (about 43x), iALS 0.0740 against 0.002516 (about
29x), Popularity 0.0012 against 0.000073: 4 of 4 rankers inflate.
MovieLens-1M: ItemKNN, EASE and iALS inflate; Popularity does not (random
0.0914 against temporal 0.1534): 3 of 4. The frozen rule needed at least 3
of 4 on each dataset.

**H3b, CONFIRMED.** The leakage audit on Arm T KuaiRec reports 0 shared
(user, item) pairs between train and test and correct time ordering
(maximum train timestamp 1598650255.432 below minimum test timestamp
1598870572.347); the planted-leak control reports 2 shared pairs and broken
time ordering and is flagged (`results/leakage_check.json`).

**H4a, REFUTED (D2-substituted rule, labelled).** Mean Kendall tau
difference against the naive estimator over 10 seeds: IPS -0.06, 95 percent
CI [-0.18, 0.06]; SNIPS -0.04, CI [-0.18, 0.08]. Neither interval excludes 0
on the positive side. In v1 this hypothesis "passed" on the void arm; on a
real arm the off-policy estimators do not beat the biased naive estimator at
ranking the five rankers. The estimand caveat of PREREGISTRATION.md section
6 applies: propensities are estimated from the within-small train pool over
the full 1,411-user grid, and the estimate is averaged over the 1,381 scored
users.

**H4b, REFUTED.** Clipped IPS (clip 10) mean tau difference -0.06, CI
[-0.20, 0.06], 10 seeds.

**S1, CONFIRMED.** Signed sampled-minus-exact bias, Popularity minus EASE,
averaged over 10 seeds: 0.0332, 95 percent CI [0.0326, 0.0337]. The metric's
bias is ranker-dependent in the predicted direction.

**H5, CONFIRMED.** Popularity-slice analysis on Arm F (slices by train
exposure): the head slice (top 1 percent) preserves the arm-wide ordering
under sampled metrics, but the torso slice (next 9 percent) reverses it
(truth: ItemKNN, Random, Popularity, EASE, iALS; sampled: Popularity, iALS,
EASE, ItemKNN, Random) and the tail slice reverses it as well (truth: iALS,
EASE, Random, ItemKNN, Popularity; sampled: Random, ItemKNN, iALS, EASE,
Popularity). At least one slice reversal satisfies the frozen rule.

**H6, CONFIRMED.** Pooled over all five rankers on Arm F (70,550
recommendations over the full 1,411-user grid): items in the top exposure
decile receive 0.2874 of recommendation slots against the 0.10 uniform
benchmark (one-proportion z = 165.93); the top 1 percent alone receives
0.2053; pooled Gini 0.7971.

**H7, CONFIRMED.** On Arm T KuaiRec, restricted to the 6,535 users whose
test positives are all cold items (no train exposure), the ItemKNN minus
Popularity gap is 0.0000106, against 0.000870 on the full test set: the
advantage is carried by warm items, as frozen (10 percent threshold).

## 5. The D2 substitution, restated

v1's H4a as literally written compares estimators against Arm F exact
metrics under a gamma = 0 logging policy that Arm F's deterministic top-10
logging cannot produce. As pre-registered in v1's DECISIONS.md D2 and
carried into this study's PREREGISTRATION.md section 6, the implemented
rule fixes gamma = 0.5 as primary for both H4a and H4b and keeps the frozen
0.05 margin. Verdicts H4a and H4b above are verdicts on the substituted
rule, and are labelled as such everywhere they appear.

## 8. Sensitivity results (frozen, reported either way)

- **Label sensitivity** (positives at watch_ratio above 1.0, truth rebuilt
  under the same discipline, 1,410 scored users): iALS 0.013778, EASE
  0.013344, Random 0.038773, ItemKNN 0.001119, Popularity 0.000000. The
  ordering among the four non-random rankers is unchanged, but Random rises
  above all learned rankers under the strict label: rewatch positives are
  close to uniformly distributed over the catalogue once training positives
  are masked. The headline ordering is therefore label-sensitive in the
  Random comparison and stable among learned rankers.
- **Pool sensitivity** (Arm F train pool restricted to events before the
  small matrix's median timestamp, 2,247,289 events): Random 0.007072,
  ItemKNN 0.000159, EASE 0.000062, iALS 0.000000, Popularity 0.000000. The
  learned rankers collapse toward zero: Arm F's measured signal is carried
  by the later part of the train window. This bounds the headline: it is a
  property of the full frozen train window, not of any prefix of it.
